Steps to Add Keys (Environment Variables):
Log in to Vercel: Go to the Vercel dashboard and select your project.
Navigate to Settings: Click the Settings tab at the top of the dashboard.
Environment Variables: On the left sidebar, click on Environment Variables.
Add Key/Value: Enter the Name (e.g., OPENAI_API_KEY) and Value (the secret key).
Select Environment: Choose which environments (Production, Preview, Development) should have access to this key.
Save: Click Save.
